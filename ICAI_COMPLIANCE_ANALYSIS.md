## ❌ NON-COMPLIANT AREAS (What Needs to Be Fixed)

### 1. Fees Mentioned on Website ❌
- **Status:** NON-COMPLIANT
- **Severity:** HIGH
- **Details:**
  - **Location 1:** `/terms-and-conditions` page - Section 3 "Professional Fees & Payment"
    - Mentions: "Fees are determined based on the complexity, scope, and time required..."
    - Mentions: "All fees are exclusive of applicable taxes (GST)"
  - **Location 2:** Contact FAQ (`/contact` page)
    - Question: "What is your fee structure?"
    - Answer mentions: "competitive pricing", "fixed fee, hourly basis, or retainer model"
- **ICAI Requirement:** **Quantum of fees (including "free of charge") must NOT be mentioned on website or advertisements**
- **Recommendation:** 
  - Remove fee-related content from Terms & Conditions page (keep only in engagement letters)
  - Remove or modify FAQ answer to state: "Fees are discussed during engagement discussions and specified in the engagement letter"

---

### 2. Solicitation Language ❌
- **Status:** NON-COMPLIANT
- **Severity:** MEDIUM-HIGH
- **Details:**
  - **Location 1:** Services page (`/services`)
    - CTA Button: "Book a consultation"
    - Text: "Schedule a call"
  - **Location 2:** Home page - WhyChooseUsSection
    - CTA Button: "Get Started"
  - **Location 3:** Services page CTA banner
    - Text: "Partner with a chartered team that moves as fast as you do"
    - Button: "Schedule a call"
- **ICAI Requirement:** **Website should not be designed in a way that actively solicits clients or work. No "contact us for lowest fees" type messaging.**
- **Recommendation:**
  - Change "Book a consultation" → "Contact Us" or "Get in Touch"
  - Change "Schedule a call" → "Contact Us" or "Reach Out"
  - Change "Get Started" → "Learn More" or "Contact Us"
  - Remove aggressive CTA language that sounds like solicitation

---

### 3. Use of Superlatives & Comparative Language ❌
- **Status:** NON-COMPLIANT
- **Severity:** MEDIUM
- **Details:**
  - **Location 1:** WhyChooseUsSection (Home page)
    - "top-notch financial services"
    - "top-notch features"
    - "Expert Advisory" (title)
  - **Location 2:** AboutUsSection (Home page)
    - "Proven Track Record"
    - "Tax Expertise" (implies specialization)
  - **Location 3:** Services page
    - "specialist pod"
    - "specialists"
  - **Location 4:** About page - AssociatesSection
    - "Specialists who extend partner-level insight"
  - **Location 5:** LeadershipSection
    - "Expert in systems, controls, and audits"
- **ICAI Requirement:** **No superlatives like "No.1 in India", "Best CA in...", "Top-rated", etc. Must not use language that implies superiority or specialization unless justified.**
- **Recommendation:**
  - Replace "top-notch" → "professional" or "quality"
  - Replace "Expert Advisory" → "Professional Advisory" or "Advisory Services"
  - Replace "Proven Track Record" → "Experience" or "Track Record"
  - Replace "Tax Expertise" → "Tax Services" or "Tax Compliance"
  - Replace "specialist/specialists" → "professional/professionals" or "team members"
  - Remove "Expert in..." → Use "Experience in..." or "Focus on..."

---

### 4. Client Statistics - Potential Testimonial Implication ❌
- **Status:** POTENTIALLY NON-COMPLIANT
- **Severity:** LOW-MEDIUM
- **Details:**
  - **Location:** StatsSection (Home page)
    - "500+ Satisfied Clients"
    - "1000+ Projects Completed"
  - **Location:** AboutHeroSection (About page)
    - "850+ Clients Served"
- **ICAI Requirement:** **No testimonials, endorsements, or "reviews from clients". Client statistics may imply satisfaction/endorsement.**
- **Recommendation:**
  - Change "Satisfied Clients" → "Clients Served" or just "Clients"
  - Consider removing client count if it appears promotional
  - Keep only factual statistics like "Years of Experience" and "Team Members"

### 5. Aggressive Marketing Language ❌
- **Status:** NON-COMPLIANT
- **Severity:** MEDIUM
- **Details:**

## 📝 NOTES

1. **Engagement Letters:** Fees can be mentioned in engagement letters (private documents), but NOT on public website
2. **Pull Model:** Website is compliant as long as no bulk emails/WhatsApp messages are sent promoting it
3. **Professional Language:** All content should be factual, dignified, and professional - avoid marketing-speak
4. **Testimonials:** Even indirect testimonials (like "Satisfied Clients") should be avoided
